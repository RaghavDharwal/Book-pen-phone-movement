Practice Mode

Drag vehicles only to valid positions. This mode checks your moves against the correct solution path. One wrong move means you lose and the board resets automatically.

Rules

  • Cars move only right (one step or jump over one bike).
  • Bikes move only left (one step or jump over one car).
  • No backward movement.
  • Wrong move => popup "You lost" + auto reset.
